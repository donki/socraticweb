## Description

sOC Scanner scans with a single button (or Enter) using any scanner Windows recognises or a camera. Put the sheet in, press the button and it appears in the preview; when you have them all, save them as a multi-page PDF, or as TIFF, JPG, PNG or BMP.

Any scanner or all-in-one printer works, over USB or the network, from the glass or the document feeder, and so does a camera: your laptop's, a webcam or a document camera, with a live view. Quality is chosen in one click (black-and-white text, grey document, colour document or photo) or set to your liking, and anything the device cannot do is adjusted automatically and explained.

For many sheets in a row there is **batch** mode: each sheet saves itself as soon as it is scanned, with a sequential name (`Invoice_0007.pdf`), never overwriting a file. It is free software, with no account, no ads and no trackers, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Main features

- Scan with one button or Enter; Esc cancels.
- Any scanner or all-in-one that Windows recognises, over USB or the network, from the glass or the feeder (all sheets in one go).
- A camera as a scanner: your laptop's, a webcam or a document camera, with live view and a choice of resolution.
- One-click quality (black-and-white text, grey document, colour document, photo) or custom: resolution, colour, page size, brightness, contrast and JPEG quality, with the megapixels and MB of each sheet shown up front.
- Anything the device cannot do is adjusted and explained: grey and black-and-white are derived from a colour image, and brightness and contrast are applied to the image when needed.
- One at a time: preview, rotate, reorder and remove sheets, and save as multi-page PDF or TIFF, or as JPG, PNG or BMP.
- Batch: each sheet saves itself with a prefix, a sequential number and a suffix; the counter carries on another day.
- PDF written by the app itself: one page per sheet, at its real size; black-and-white in lossless 1 bit (a page of text takes a few tens of KB).
- When done: open, show in folder or copy the file to paste it into an email or a chat.
- Errors are explained with the reason and what to do: out of paper, jam, lid open, busy, switched off, camera permission, file in use, disk full…

## User guide (support)

### Installation

1. Install it from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N9S5ZP2SG54), which keeps it up to date for you.
2. Or download the `sOCScanner.exe` executable from the GitHub releases page (https://github.com/donki/Scanner/releases) and double-click it: there is nothing to install. If Windows warns that the publisher is unknown, click «More info» and «Run anyway».

Requirements: Windows 10 (version 2004) or later, or Windows 11, 64-bit. The scanner has to be added in Windows (Printers & scanners) with the manufacturer's driver.

### First run

The first time, the «Setup guide» opens, step by step (Back / Next at the bottom): your scanner or camera in Windows (with a button to Printers & scanners), the camera permission (only if you use one), the quality, and the batch folder and names. Each step says whether it is done, pending or optional. Open it again whenever you like with the help button (?).

### Main window

- **Scanner or camera**: the list of devices, with the refresh button. Below, what the chosen one supports (glass, feeder, resolutions) and, if it has both, whether to scan from the glass or the feeder.
- **Quality**: the ready-made quality and, with the gear button, the dialog to set your own.
- **How to work**: One at a time or Batch, with the format suggested when saving or, in batch mode, the folder, the format and the name of the next sheet.
- **Preview**: the selected sheet in large and, below, all the scanned ones, with buttons to rotate, remove and move them.
- **Bottom**: the scan button (Enter), Save and Discard, and what happened with the last sheet.
- **Top right**: Guide, What's new, Español / English and About.

### Custom quality

The gear next to the quality opens a dialog: resolution (only those the chosen device supports), colour, grey or black-and-white, page size (A4, Letter, A5 or the whole glass), brightness, contrast and JPEG quality (also used for photos inside the PDF). At the bottom you see the megapixels per sheet and its size before compression.

### Batch

Choose the folder, the format and the name: what goes before and after the number, the next number and its digits. Each sheet saves itself as soon as it is scanned, and the name of the next one is shown. If a number already exists it skips to the next free one: a file is never overwritten.

## Frequently asked questions

**My scanner is not in the list.**
Add it in Windows (Settings › Bluetooth & devices › Printers & scanners) with the manufacturer's driver, switch it on and press the refresh button. The setup guide has a button that opens that page.

**The same printer appears twice.**
Once over USB and once over the network. Both work; USB usually gives a higher resolution.

**Why can't I choose 1200 dpi?**
Only the resolutions the chosen device supports are listed. If you ask for a quality it cannot reach, it is set to the highest it has and you are told.

**The camera does not turn on.**
Windows has to let desktop apps use the camera: Settings › Privacy & security › Camera › «Let desktop apps access your camera». The guide opens it.

**How big is a sheet?**
A black-and-white page of text takes a few tens of KB as PDF. The quality dialog shows the megapixels and MB before scanning.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your PC: scans in the folder you choose, the settings in your profile and an error log with no personal data. The app uses the scanner and the camera only when you choose them and scan. It does not connect to the internet or upload anything. No account, no ads, no trackers, no analytics.
