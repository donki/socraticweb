## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information from one visit
to the next. This policy complies with Article 22.2 of Ley 34/2002 de servicios de la sociedad de la
información y de comercio electrónico (LSSI-CE, Spanish Information Society Services and Electronic
Commerce Act) and with the guide on the use of cookies issued by the Agencia Española de Protección
de Datos (AEPD, Spanish Data Protection Agency).

## 2. Who sets cookies on this website

This website is hosted on **WordPress.com**, a service of Automattic Inc. We **do not set any cookies
of our own**, nor have we added analytics, advertising, pixels or trackers. The cookies that are
present are set by the platform in order to work and for its statistics.

## 3. Cookies in use

- **`eucookielaw`** — *technical, from this website, lasts 1 year.* Remembers that you have closed
  the cookie notice so that it is not shown again.
- **`tk_ai`** — *analytics, from WordPress.com (Automattic), lasts about 13 months.* Anonymous
  identifier for the platform's usage statistics.
- **`tk_qs`** — *analytics, from WordPress.com (Automattic), lasts a few minutes.* Briefly stores the
  events for those statistics before sending them.

If you are signed in to WordPress.com with your own account, your browser also sends the cookies for
that session, which are technical and depend on your account, not on this website.

In addition, to display the page, your browser requests resources from Automattic servers
(`s0.wp.com`, `fonts.wp.com`, `stats.wp.com`, `pixel.wp.com` and `gravatar.com`). As with any web
request, those servers receive your IP address and the page you are visiting.

## 4. How to accept, reject or delete them

- **Cookie notice:** the first time you visit, you will see a notice. Clicking "Accept" closes it,
  and it will not appear again for a year.
- **Rejecting them:** the website works the same without cookies. You can block those from
  `wordpress.com`, or all third-party cookies, in your browser settings:
  [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en),
  [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09),
  [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox),
  [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac).
- **Withdrawing consent:** delete this site's cookies from those same settings.
