## 9. This website

The sections above deal with the apps. This is what happens when you visit this website.

- **Data controller:** Josep, developer of sOCratic. Contact:
  [jsoladelarosa@gmail.com](mailto:jsoladelarosa@gmail.com).
- **What data is collected:** none through the website. There are no forms, sign-ups, newsletters or
  comments (comments are closed across the whole site).
- **If you send us an email:** we use your address and what you tell us only to reply to you. The
  legal basis is the consent you give by writing to us and our legitimate interest in handling your
  enquiry (Article 6.1(a) and (f) of the Reglamento General de Protección de Datos, RGPD, General
  Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). It is kept for as long as the conversation lasts and for the
  time needed to deal with any subsequent claim, and it is not disclosed to anyone.
- **Hosting and statistics:** the website is on WordPress.com (Automattic Inc.), which, as the
  provider, records technical data about each visit (IP address, browser, page visited) in order to
  serve it, protect it and compile visit statistics. The cookies it uses are listed in the
  [cookie policy](https://socraticweb0.wordpress.com/en/cookies/).
- **Your rights:** you can request access to, rectification, erasure, objection to, restriction of
  processing and portability of your data by writing to the contact email. If you believe they have
  not been respected, you can file a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos
  (AEPD, Spanish Data Protection Agency) ([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).
